# Toon on Set · 实景卡通 — decisions

Made from a blank page. "Base" is the technique base, Tilt-Shift Miniature (`tilt-shift`); "—" means new. Borrowed: Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon (`scifi-toon`), 2.5D Pixel Art (`hd-2d`).

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use / size | 1920×1080 landscape | 1080×1920 vertical | YouTube Shorts for a comedy story channel |
| §1 Essence | a real place made to look like a model by lens and clock | flat 2D cartoon actors inside a full-size, stylised but believable 3D set, sharing one light | the reference's look; the user wants no toy/LEGO feel |
| §2 Vantage | high oblique, 45–55° pitch, 100–200 m | camera at the characters' eye level, pitch ±8°, 30–50 mm | film-like comedy staging |
| §2 Blur | tilt-shift band + physical DOF | physical DOF only, focused on the face or prop; no screen-space AO | shallow focus without the miniature trick |
| §2 Materials | photoreal town, facade shader | canvas-painted metal, wood, gravel, concrete with wear; soft bevels; no studs, no plastic | "stylised but believable"; not LEGO |
| §2 Characters | small colour capsules / lathe ducks | flat vector cartoon cards: round white head, thick constant-width outline, stick limbs, flat clothes, hard shadow shape (from scifi-toon, no boil) | the reference's cast grammar, in the spirit of the user's "TÔI" |
| §2 Cast in light | — | lit billboard + sun-facing shadow card, contact shadow (from hd-2d, no pixels) | the cartoon casts a real shadow in the set |
| §3 Colour | saturated model-railway paint | warm cinematic afternoon sun, 2–3 strong painted set colours on natural neutrals; white heads, one flat clothes colour per character | reads as a film set, the cast pops |
| §4 Type | in-world sign panels, time-lapse clock | word-by-word white heavy sans (Baloo 2 ExtraBold) with black outline, centred lower-middle; no clock | Shorts captions, readable with sound off |
| §4 Caption language | — | English by default | the user's channel language (style-frame note) |
| §5 Motion | time-lapse stutter (8 Hz world), smooth camera | normal timing: cast pose-to-pose at 12 fps, camera and set at 30 fps; squash & stretch | comedy acting, not time-lapse |
| §6 Camera | high wides, dives, rises, band racks | eye-level medium, low hero angle, reaction close-up, insert, snap zoom, OTS, rack focus | film comedy grammar |
| §7 Sound | minimalist tuned percussion gated by events | light comedy score (pizzicato, ukulele, marimba, bassoon) + cartoon SFX on the action, silence before the punchline, narrator | user's choice: "Hài nhẹ + SFX bật" |
| §8 Native moves | speed drop, band as pointer, god's-eye system… | real shadow; absurd-scale reveal; question → demo → reaction; the insert; crowd of cards; snap zoom on the punchline | moves for "Why do they…" shorts |
