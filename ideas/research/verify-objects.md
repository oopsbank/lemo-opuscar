## Items to handle carefully
- **Myth (popular claim false):** 23 (toothpaste squares); 8 ("brushes clean your shoes").
- **Contested:** 12 (why 21 crimps), 24 (10:10), 31 (pink erasers), 52 (bobby pins), 60 (yellow pencils).
- **Popular answer wrong or overstated:** 1 (not anti-drying), 3 ("fifth pocket"), 4 (main *entry* floor, not always the lobby), 5 (wood savings), 10 (sole-inventor credit), 18 (spoon rest is a hack), 20 (pump hose is not the indicator), 22 (choking claim), 26 (swatch for detergent testing), 28 (stop signs were yellow), 29 (pleats for batter expansion), 33 (holes are not mainly for mortar), 34 (12 pentagons forced, 20 hexagons not), 37 (flag means outgoing mail), 38 (many covers aren't round), 40 ("just air to cheat you"), 43 (1.24× figure), 44 (Scanlon 1914 is wrong; it was about 1940), 45 (snow reason), 53 (not a battery), 56 (cat legend), 58 (Levitt), 59 (ferrous vs ferric; blood myth).
- **Child-safety flag:** 30 (hood drawstrings on kids' clothing are restricted in the US).
