# Pixel Life — idea backlog

2.5D pixel-art dioramas (`styles/hd-2d`), 55–61 s (test one ~40 s cut: see README §6), "You Are a
…" job comedies where everything goes wrong; the last line echoes the first, so the film loops.
Pixel sprites, so no realistic humans. Research and ranking method: [`README.md`](README.md).

Already made: *Fish Seller*, *Pizza Delivery Guy*, *Night Museum Guard*. So: **no food delivery,
no museum, no fish stall, no aliens.** Pocket City owns vehicles/town emergencies; Pixel Life stays
inside one job, one set, one person.

Format reminders: hook = "You are a <job>." + the first thing already going wrong in frame 1;
three escalating disasters; a dialogue-box line from a customer/boss; final line echoes the first
with a twist ("You are a lighthouse keeper. …You were a lighthouse keeper."). End with a question
that invites comments ("Which job next?").

Score = Viral × Feasible (each 1–5).

## Top 10

1. **You Are a Hotel Receptionist** — V5 × F5 = 25
   - Story: One desk, one bell, twelve keys: a guest wants a sea view (there is no sea), a
     honeymoon couple gets the room with the goat, the lift opens on the wrong floor every time,
     a tour group of 40 arrives at once.
   - Hook: "You are a hotel receptionist." *DING DING DING DING* — the bell already being hammered.
   - Why: universally known job, sound gag in frame 1, comments full of real hotel stories.
2. **You Are a Lighthouse Keeper** — V5 × F5 = 25
   - Story: One job — keep the light on. The bulb blows, the stairs have 300 steps, the spare is at
     the bottom, a ship is coming, and a seagull lives in the lamp room.
   - Hook: The beam sweeps, flickers, dies; "You are a lighthouse keeper. You had one job."
   - Why: lights + storm are what this style does best; "you had one job" is a meme everyone knows.
3. **You Are a Barista** — V5 × F4 = 20
   - Story: The order: "oat, half-caf, extra-hot, no foam, with foam on the side"; the machine
     erupts, the latte art becomes a monster, the queue reaches the door.
   - Hook: A ticket printer spitting out an endless order.
   - Why: hugely relatable, order-absurdity comments ("this is my order"). No brand: an invented café.
4. **You Are a Wizard's Apprentice (Cleaner)** — V4 × F5 = 20
   - Story: Your job is only to dust the tower; you dust a wand; the brooms start helping; the
     books fly; the master returns in 10 seconds.
   - Hook: "You are a wizard's cleaner. Rule one: don't touch the wand." — touches the wand.
   - Why: fantasy fits pixel RPG fans; magic VFX glow. (Avoid any famous mouse-and-brooms look:
     original wizard, original gags.)
5. **You Are a Zookeeper** — V5 × F4 = 20
   - Story: Feeding time: the penguins escape, the giraffe eats your hat, the lion is asleep on the
     key, a school group arrives.
   - Hook: An open gate and a line of tiny penguin sprites waddling out.
   - Why: animals are the most shareable characters; sprite animals are simple.
6. **You Are a Ski Lift Operator** — V4 × F5 = 20
   - Story: One button: start/stop. The chairs swing, a dog rides alone, a snowboarder is stuck at
     the top, the snow buries the hut.
   - Hook: Lift stopped, 20 people dangling, a hand over a big red button.
   - Why: snow scene in the style's own colour examples; winter release.
7. **You Are a Librarian** — V4 × F5 = 20
   - Story: Silence is the job: a phone rings in every aisle, a toddler finds the drum books, a
     book returned 40 years late, the shush that knocks over the shelves.
   - Hook: "Shhh." The tiniest sound, everyone freezes — then a book falls.
   - Why: quiet-to-chaos comedy, cosy amber lamps, comment bait about overdue books.
8. **You Are a Night Train Conductor** — V4 × F4 = 16
   - Story: A sleeper train: tickets in the wrong bunks, a snoring passenger wakes the carriage, a
     goose in first class, a tunnel blackout.
   - Hook: "Tickets, please" — a sleeping passenger rolls over and falls into the aisle.
   - Why: corridor set is a simple box diorama with window lights; dark + warm look.
9. **You Are a Lifeguard** — V4 × F4 = 16
   - Story: The pool is empty all summer until the one minute you get a sandwich; an inflatable
     flamingo armada, a cannonball contest, the deep-end drain.
   - Hook: Whistle blow, a flood of inflatables leaping in at once.
   - Why: summer colours, water shader with light columns, physical comedy.
10. **You Are a Dentist** — V4 × F4 = 16
    - Story: A patient who won't open, a drill that sounds like a rocket, a tooth that flies across
      the room, a nervous dad who faints.
    - Hook: Close on a pixel mouth: "Open wide." It closes.
    - Why: everyone has a dentist story; slightly cringe = comments.

## Notes

- Strongest title pattern: `You Are a <Job>` (searchable job noun first) + a twist subtitle in the
  description. Put the job in the first spoken line and in big text in frame 1.
- Pair seasonal jobs with the calendar: Ski Lift (Dec–Feb), Mall Santa (Dec), Haunted House (Oct),
  Lifeguard (Jun–Aug).
- End card question: "What job should I try next?" turns comments into the backlog.
