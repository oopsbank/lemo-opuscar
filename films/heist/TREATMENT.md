# The Last Train Heist (Chuyến tàu 00:00) — treatment

The user's own script: 65 s, 9:16, wordless, SFX only (no music), Tilt-Shift Miniature style, night.

Logline: a tiny thief blows the bank door, gets boxed in by police, and escapes over the rooftops onto the midnight train.

## Three structures considered
1. **One map, three tiers, the camera only breathes, zooms and follows (the script's own grammar)**: bottom = start (bank), middle = the chase (junction, rooftops), top = the goal (station, train, tunnel). The glowing turquoise bag is the eye-line through the whole film. **Chosen.**
2. Cut between close-ups (thief, police, clock): faster, but it breaks the "whole route on one toy map" pleasure.
3. Start on the train leaving and rewind: spoils the jump.

## Colour plan (from the script)
Everything cold and dark (night blue, grey). Only three loud light sources: the turquoise bag (the hero), police red/blue, the train's warm yellow (the goal). Street lamps switch to flashing red with the alarm.

## Map (world metres, x east, z south toward the camera)
- Top: an elevated railway along z −20 (deck 7 m up); the station on the left with an LED clock 23:59; a 4-car train with warm windows at the platform; a dark tunnel mouth in a cliff block on the right (x 13).
- Middle: the junction at (0, 3) with traffic lights and 7 civilian cars; three 4-storey blocks in a row north of it (x 5–13, z −0.6 to −17), roofs with tanks, laundry racks, AC units; a fire escape on the first block's south face; on the last roof a giant neon billboard leaning 45° toward the rails.
- Bottom: a quiet street with round toy trees; the domed bank (front door on the street); a café with a red-white awning, 3 tables, customers; an alley up to the junction.

## Beats (film seconds; a 0.3× slow-motion at the top of the jump)
0–6 the bank door blows (a round cartoon puff), the thief runs out with the glowing bag; the street lamps flash red; people freeze and turn.
6–20 he runs for the junction; 6 police cars rush in from the four corners and box it; a helicopter comes in from top left, its searchlight sweeping; civilian cars brake and bump; the café customers run inside. He turns back, into the alley.
20–36 push in: across the road, up the fire escape, across the first roof, jump, round the tanks, jump; the searchlight at his heels; the clock turns 00:00, doors hiss, the train's headlight comes on.
36–50 the train pulls out toward the tunnel; he sprints up the leaning billboard and leaps; slow motion at the top (heartbeat, silence); he lands on the last car, rolls, grabs the rail; the train dives into the tunnel, one last turquoise flash.
50–65 pull back to the whole map: a long useless line of police under the viaduct, lights still flashing; the helicopter hangs there, lost; an orange street sweeper potters into the empty junction, brushes spinning. Black.
