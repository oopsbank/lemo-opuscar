# The Ring Run — treatment

The user's script (from the "Kịch bản" session, confirmed by the user): 72 s, 9:16, wordless, on-screen text in English, Tilt-Shift Miniature, daylight pastel.
Logline: a pigeon knocks the wedding ring off the hotel roof; the groom has one minute to chase it through three tiers of the city before the chapel bell strikes noon.

## Structures considered
1. **One hillside map, three tiers stacked in the vertical frame, hard cuts between shot types (the script's grammar)**: the clock tower rises through every wide as the countdown. **Chosen.**
2. A single continuous crane through the whole chase: elegant, but loses the ECU beats (peck, fish, catch).
3. Start at the altar and flash back: kills the countdown.

## Staging
- Map: hotel roof y 22 (railing, ring box, laundry line, green fire escape, GRAND HOTEL sign); market street y 8 (café awning, stalls, tram 7 rails curving north at x 8, the manhole); terrace wall with an open mossy pipe channel and stone steps; canal with a stone bridge, the fisherman, the flower boat "JUST MARRIED"; the white chapel whose roof lifts off for the dollhouse shots, and its clock tower (11:59 → 12:00).
- The ring is the only gold, the groom's bow the only red; a star sparkle and a gold trail mark every flight.
- One ring timeline (story.js `ringPos`) drives every shot; the golden trail at 62–67 s replays it.
- Text: "THE WEDDING STARTS IN 1 MINUTE." (2–4.6 s) and "NOT AGAIN." (70–71 s). The last second replays the fall at 2 s, so the video loops.
- Sound: sparse toy foley with real silences; a music-box chase (glockenspiel, pizzicato, ukulele, CC0) speeding up; only the chapel bell is full size.
