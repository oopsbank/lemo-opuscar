# Tunnel or Bridge? One Ambulance Each — treatment

Pocket City, Pick a Side #2 (idea #2 in ideas/pocket-city.md). 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~32 s, English VO (Kokoro am_michael), golden hour.

## The question
A patient on the ferry quay; the hospital is just across the canal. Two identical ambulances leave at the same moment:
**A (blue roof) takes the tunnel** under the canal, the longer way round; **B (orange roof) takes the drawbridge**, the short way.
Who gets to the hospital bay first? The viewer picks a side; the comments argue.

## Three structures considered
1. **Split screen, both routes at once.** Two half-height views the whole time. Fair, but every shot is half size, the tilt-shift look shrinks to letterboxes, and the post pass would have to run twice per frame. Rejected.
2. **Pick a side, chronological** (like Two Tugs): start line, race, finish. Same skeleton as the previous film: the playbook forbids it twice in a row.
3. **Result first (chosen).** Frame 0 is the finish: both ambulances, sirens on, converging on the same hospital bay from opposite ends of the street, metres apart. Headline WHO GETS THERE FIRST? A rewind whoosh and a "40 SECONDS EARLIER" stamp at 1.6 s; the race plays out with cuts alternating A/B and a route-map inset showing both dots; the end returns to the finish in ×¼ slow motion and the tunnel wins by half a bonnet.

## Skeleton and opening (rotation log)
- Skeleton: **Result first** (previous: Pick a side).
- Opening type: **(b) result first** — two vehicles converging at the lens (previous: Chase → Reveal).
- Frame 0: ambulance A big in the foreground (≈ 40% of the height), B behind it in the other lane, the hospital canopy; sirens and a horn hit on frame 0.

Payoff fact (true, ≤ 12 words): a tunnel is never closed to let a boat through; a movable bridge is.

## Where
- Hospital: west of the main street, just north of the canal (the Phantom Jam hospital block, x −64…−18, z 566…612; bay at x −13.5, z 592).
- Start: the ferry quay, quay road z 746, x −20.
- B (bridge): east along the quay road → north over the drawbridge at x 120 → west along z 630 → north up x 0 → bay. ≈ 430 m.
- A (tunnel): west along the quay road → north into a new immersed-tube tunnel at x −130 (portals at z ≈ 712 and z ≈ 598) → north to z 520 → east → south down x 0 → bay. ≈ 520 m.
- Converge at the bay: A from the north, B from the south on the same street.
