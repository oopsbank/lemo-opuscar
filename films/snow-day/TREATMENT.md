# Snow Day — treatment

Style: Tilt-Shift Miniature. Pocket City #4, YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 36 s, smooth motion. English voice (Kokoro `am_michael`) and subtitles. Same harbour town, under a rare overnight snowfall. Idea #4 from the research backlog ("Midnight Snow, 6 a.m. Opening"), moved to 6:00 → 8:00 so the light stays a low winter sun throughout.

Research applied: hook in frame 1 (the clock 06:00, the line "Two hours. Three ploughs." on screen from frame 0, a plough already moving); a change every 2–4 s; a seamless loop (the last shot rises back to the opening aerial while fresh snow covers the roads again, and the last line "Hold on. Is that snow?" leads into the first); music with no final cadence; one hidden gag (a snowman in the park wearing an orange cone, the ploughs' colour).

## Three candidate structures
1. **Race the clock (chosen).** Three ploughs, two hours, every road; one plough stuck (real time); the last road to the school cleared at 7:59:59; the bell; it starts snowing again (loop).
2. **One plough's night.** Follow a single plough street by street. Intimate, but loses the satisfying whole-grid reveal.
3. **Before/after split.** The town white, then a hard cut to black streets. Cheap; it skips the process people watch for.

## Logline and arc
Snow buries Pocket City overnight; you have two hours and three ploughs to open every road before school starts at eight.
Setup (06:00, white town, P1 rolls out of the fire station) → progress (boulevard, the grid drawn in black from above) → the obstacle (the blue plough stuck in a drift; real time; back up and ram it) → the last road (7:50, the school street) → real time 7:59:57 → the plough pushes the last pile to the school gate; bell at 8:00:00; kids cheer → the camera rises back to the opening aerial; snow again (loop).

## Benchmark
Tilt-shift time-lapse films (learn: the speed drop on one detail, the system seen from above; take nothing). Snow-clearing footage from above (learn: the pattern of black lanes appearing). Don't take shots or music.

## Cue map and sound
D major, marimba pulse, pizzicato bass, sleigh bells, glockenspiel hook; brass and snare build in shot 5. Tape-stop into silence at both real-time drops (14.5 and 27.5): only diesel engines, wheel spin, reverse beeps, clock ticks. Timpani roll into the ram, crash on the burst. Bell at 8:00 releases the full theme; no final cadence (loop).
Ambience: soft winter wind, muffled town; plough diesels panned; blade scrape; at the end a snowfall hush.
