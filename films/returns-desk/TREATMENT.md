# You Are a Returns Desk Clerk — Pixel Life, Job #5

2.5D pixel art (styles/hd-2d), vertical 1080×1920, 60 fps, **32 s**, English. The recurring hero (lime jacket) works a
department store's returns desk on 26 December. Three returns, each worse than the last; the queue swallows the store; he is
fired, then rehired because nobody else applied, just as The Customer comes back. The last line runs into the first.

**Visual upgrade (from this episode on):** a dense set with things going on in the background; practical light that moves; squash
and stretch, reaction faces, particles, screen shake and a short freeze on every gag; a chunky pixel UI; text and a gag on frame 0.

## The idea in one line
*"I'd like to return this. It's unopened." — a cake with a slice missing.*

## Benchmarks (learn / don't take)
- **Angry Retail Guy (Scott Seiss)**: the customer's absurd line comes first, the worker's deadpan answer second. Learn: the cold
  open on the line, the flat reply. Don't take: his face-to-camera monologue; ours is a sight-gag world.
- **Epic NPC Man**: game logic applied to a day job. Learn: HUD and quest banner as jokes. Don't take: any of their characters.
- **Tati's *Playtime*** (the department store set): many small actions in deep space at once. Learn: background life that
  rewards a rewatch. Don't take: its long takes; ours cuts every 2–5 s.

## Three structures
- **A. Three customers at the desk, one locked frame.** Cheapest; reads like the hotel film. Rejected: the brief asks for more
  space and life, and a locked frame hides the store.
- **B. "The queue is the villain."** The desk in close-up for two returns, then a crane up and back that reveals the queue
  snaking through the store and out into the snowy car park; back in for the avalanche. **Chosen:** each gag has its own camera
  move (a slam, a pan along the receipt, a reveal, a collapse), the set earns its detail, and the reveal is the big laugh.
- **C. One continuous dolly along the queue, from the car park to the desk.** Strong for the set, but the first gag would come
  too late for a cold open.

## Look
- **Set:** a department store, the returns desk at the back. Wall: a lit RETURNS lightbox, NOW SERVING display, posters
  ("NO RECEIPT? NO PROBLEM*"), a SALE −70% banner, drooping post-Christmas tinsel and baubles. Behind the hero, a wire returns cage
  piled with returned things (a giant teddy, a lamp, ugly jumpers, boxes). Desk: till with a green screen glow, receipt printer,
  bell, ticket dispenser, a small potted plant, the Cat's tray. Left: high shelves of boxes, a staffer on a ladder. Right: the glass
  doors, snow and daylight outside, a coin-op rocket ride with a kid. Foreground: queue stanchions with belts, out of focus.
- **Light:** fluorescent ceiling tubes (emissive + point lights, one flickering over the desk), warm desk lamp, cool daylight
  through the doors with dust in the beam, contact shadows. Grade: calm cool morning → hot, harsh noon as the queue grows →
  red during GAME OVER → morning again for the loop.
- **Animation:** squash and stretch on slams, pops and landings; reaction faces (stare, sweat, shock, sad); emote pops ("!", "?",
  sweat drop, anger vein); particles (crumbs, coins, paper, dust, snow); screen shake + 0.1 s freeze + flash on the four big hits.
- **UI:** Pixelify Sans and Silkscreen (OFL); hard-edged boxes, two-tone pixel borders, offset drop shadows, pop-in with overshoot.
