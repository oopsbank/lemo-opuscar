# FLOOR -99 — treatment

Style: **16-bit Pixel RPG** (`styles/pixel-rpg`) pushed toward action-horror, vertical 9:16.
- **Picture:** a 180×320 indexed framebuffer ×6 → 1080×1920, 24 fps, 65 s.
- **Language:** 100 % English on screen and in the voice (the user's spec).
- **Engine:** the one from CA ĐÊM (px.js, font.js).

The script is the user's: every beat, line, UI text and timing below is theirs. I direct the shots, motion, sound and rhythm.

**Departures from STYLE.md, at the user's request**
- **CRT overlay:** grid-aligned scanline gaps, a vignette and noise pixels; never a blur.
- **Heavy screen shake:** integer-pixel shake, up to 4 art px.
- **Colour:** high-contrast blue/red.
- **Protected colours:** the flare/alarm red and the white of the eyes and claws.

## Three structures
- **A. The user's script (chosen).** A descent in six levels: free fall → B13 claws → the faceless guard → the flood → the shaft chase → the exit that isn't. Each level answers with an item (spray, screwdriver, flare, brake): the RPG inventory as a survival kit. The ending turns the whole film into one loop.
- **B. A floor counter as the spine.** The LED number as a constant HUD counting down with every scene. Rejected as a structure but kept as a motif: the LED reappears in the cabin, the shaft HUD and the final sign.
- **C. A battle-screen version.** Each monster as a turn-based fight. Rejected: the user wants real-time action.

## Logline and arc
Leo survives a falling elevator through five floors of horrors with what's in his backpack, but the "exit" at the bottom welcomes him to Floor -99: Loop 01 complete.
- **Setup:** the cable snaps.
- **Turn:** escape upward through the hatch.
- **Ending:** the safe exit is the start of the loop.

## Cue map
- **Tempo:** 140 bpm (beat 0.4286 s).
- **0–4:** no music: the siren, metal shriek, the snap.
- **4–15:** a pulsing bass ostinato (a triangle, A minor), noise hats, dissonant stabs on the hits.
- **15–27:** a drone with a detuned pulse pad; the neon hum. The music drops out on the head turn.
- **27–40:** the bass returns with fast arpeggios; water rush; drops for the pop.
- **40–53:** the full chase (kick/snare noise drums, bass, a square lead riff).
- **53–55:** the music stops dead on the brake.
- **55–59.3:** a warm major pad (C major) and the TING.
- **59.3–63.5:** the pad detunes down and the bit-crush grows.
- **63.5–65:** static, then zero.

## Type and UI
- **UI windows:** black with a coloured 1-px border.
  - Red for warnings (WARNING, OXYGEN), blinking at 2 Hz.
  - White for the floor label.
  - Yellow for ITEM USED.
- **Dialog box:** black with a white border; LEO's name tag; text typed with blips.
- **Ghost text:** letters jittering ±1 px, with a random wrong glyph flashing.
- **CRITICAL HIT!:** big ×2 outlined text, red/yellow, popping up and floating.
- **The final sign:** a pixel exit-sign panel, green then glitching red.
