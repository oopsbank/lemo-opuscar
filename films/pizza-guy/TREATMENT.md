# You Are a Pizza Delivery Guy — treatment

Style: 2.5D Pixel Art (`styles/hd-2d/STYLE.md`). Vertical 1080×1920, 60 fps, English voice and subtitles, ~61 s.
Brief (same channel and set-up as the previous short): you, in the second person, deliver one pizza on a rainy night; one situation after another; narrator + in-world dialogue boxes; no logo; no storyboard stop.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. The rule book.** Narrator reads delivery rules and each breaks at once. *Rejected: it's the previous short's structure; the channel needs variety.*

**B. The overworld map.** A top-down town map with a route line; the camera dives into each stop and pulls back to the map between them. *Strong RPG grammar, but the map trips cost 4–5 s each, and in 60 s that is time not spent on gags.*

**C. The countdown run (chosen).** One pizza, one address, thirty minutes. A small pocket-watch gauge at the top of the frame drains as you ride down one long rainy street, and each stop on the way costs you time. The pizza box itself glows warm while it's hot (the carried light as a meter), dimming stop by stop. Situations: a dog chases you; a gust throws the pizza onto your face; you knock at No. 13 (a ghost) instead of No. 31; you reach No. 31, a bear in a bathrobe, and the watch runs out one second before he opens. Late means free, so he shuts the door, and you eat the pizza on his porch step with the dog that chased you.

Why C: a countdown gives drive to a chain of unrelated gags, and the address mix-up (13 / 31) pays off the order card. The dog is the running gag and becomes the warm ending. Opening image from the topic: a dark wet street where a neon PIZZA sign stutters on. Score shape from the topic: a tarantella (mandolin tremolo, guitar, recorder, tambourine) that keeps getting knocked off its feet.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** On a rainy night you have thirty minutes to deliver one pizza, and the town throws a dog, a gust, a ghost and a bear at you, so you end up sharing it with the dog.

- **Setup** (0–7.5 s): the neon PIZZA sign stutters on in the rain. "You are a pizza delivery guy." ORDER card: one pizza, No. 31, thirty minutes. The watch starts.
- **Escalation** (7.5–35 s): the dog chases the scooter; on the bridge a gust lifts the lid and the pizza lands on your face; you knock at No. 13, lightning strikes, and a ghost answers.
- **Turn** (35–47 s): No. 31, a log cabin. The porch lamp clicks on and a bear in a bathrobe opens the door just as the watch runs out: LATE. "You're one second late. So it's free. Keep it." The door slams.
- **Ending** (47–61 s): you sit on the step and open the box; the dog sits beside you; the rain stops. "One pizza. Thirty minutes. One very happy customer." Rising pull-back over the whole wet town, title.

## 3. Benchmark

- *A Short Hike* (adamgryu, 2019): **learn** a tiny pixel figure in a lit 3D world, warm small lights in a cold big place, gentle comedy from small encounters. **Don't take** characters, the island, music or UI.
- Pixar's *Partly Cloudy* (2009): **learn** escalating comic hazards to a courier and a warm ending between two unlikely friends. **Don't take** characters, designs, shots or music.

## 4. Characters and sets

- **You**: red cap and red jacket (the saturated colour, unused by any set), on a little red scooter with a glowing pizza box on the rack. Poses: ride (wheel frames), brake, stand with box, knock, shock, pizza-face, run, sit with box, eat, share.
- **The Dog**: small scruffy tan mutt with one floppy ear. Poses: sleep, bark, run (4), sit, beg, eat, wag.
- **The Ghost**: a sheet ghost with a lantern-green glow, floating; poses: float (2), peek, wave.
- **The Bear**: big, in a striped bathrobe and slippers, holding a mug; poses: stand, look down, talk, point, shut.
- **Set: Crooked Lane, one long street on a rainy night.** West to east: the pizza shop (neon sign, steamy window), terraced houses with lit windows, the dog's kennel, a stone bridge over a canal, No. 13 (crooked, dark, a green-lit window, a dead tree, an iron fence), a lamp-lit gap, No. 31 (a log cabin with a porch, a porch lamp and a step). Wet cobbles that catch every light. Key: cold blue rain ambient; practicals: neon (pink-red), windows (amber), scooter headlight (white), the hot box (warm orange), ghost window (green), porch lamp (amber), lightning (white-blue). At the end the rain stops and stars come out.

## 7. Cue map

- **Tempo**: 6/8, dotted-quarter 120 (bar = 1 s); the flee from No. 13 doubles the drum rate; the finale at the same tempo with half-time feel.
- **Instruments**: mandolin tremolo (pluck.py pipa preset with tremolo), guitar (acoustic), recorder, tambourine + hand drum, pizzicato bass; harp and celesta for the ghost and the finale; a synth horn for the LATE stinger.
- **Sections**: intro rain (0–3) · tarantella A (3–18.5, dead stop on the splat) · soft vamp (22.5–27) · silence (27–28.5) · ghost (28.5–32, drone + celesta) · flee (32–35) · hesitant pizzicato (35–39) · LATE stinger (39) · near-silence (41–48) · finale (50–56) · title chord (56).
- Cards, banners, dialogue boxes, the splat, the flash, the lamp, LATE, the slam and the title all land on beats; `tools/cuecheck.py` checks them.
