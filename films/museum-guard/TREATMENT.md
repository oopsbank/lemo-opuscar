# You Are a Night Museum Guard — treatment

Style: 2.5D Pixel Art (`styles/hd-2d/STYLE.md`). Vertical 1080×1920, 60 fps, English voice and subtitles, ~61 s.
Brief (Pixel Life, film 3; same set-up as the first two): you, in the second person, work one night shift in a small museum; one situation after another; narrator + in-world dialogue boxes; no logo; no storyboard stop; many situations.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. The clock rounds.** A wall clock card for each hour (midnight, 1, 2, 3 a.m.) and one room per hour. *Rejected: a clock running down is film 2's countdown again.*

**B. The patrol log.** The guard's checklist ("Dinosaur: present. Paintings: quiet.") ticked off and broken item by item. *Rejected: a list that breaks is film 1's rule book.*

**C. The floor plan (chosen).** The museum is one diorama with its ceiling off, seen first from high above as a floor plan, a single flashlight cone moving through the dark rooms. The camera dives from the plan into one room per situation and the film ends on the same plan at dawn, changed. The flashlight is the carried light (the style's "carried light as a meter"): it is the key light of every room, it flickers as the night goes on, and when it dies the film goes black. Then the exhibits switch their own display lights on one by one around you and a single candle replaces the torch.

Why C: the floor plan is the style's "menu of places" structure, new for the channel, and a museum *is* a map of rooms. The dead torch gives the film a real dark beat and a turn that is warm instead of scary: it's your birthday, and nobody remembered except the exhibits. Opening image from the topic: a dark floor plan with one moving cone of light. Score shape from the topic: a tiptoe pizzicato march (bassoon, celesta) that freezes whenever you turn round, then breaks into a small waltz for the party.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** On a night shift where "nothing ever happens", the dinosaur moves when you're not looking, a duchess in a painting talks back, a mummy wants five more centuries, and when your torch dies the whole museum throws you a surprise birthday party.

- **Setup** (0–8 s): the floor plan, the cone of light. "You are a night museum guard." NIGHT SHIFT card: "Nothing ever happens here."
- **Escalation** (8–37.5 s): Hall of Giants: the dinosaur skeleton plays "grandmother's footsteps" behind you. Portrait Gallery: the duchess tells you to lower your light, and wins the argument. Egypt Room: a mummy sits up, asks if it's morning, and goes back to sleep.
- **Turn** (37.5–45 s): back in the Hall the torch flickers and dies. Black, silence, a heartbeat, "H-hello?" One by one the display lights click on: the dinosaur, the mummy holding a cake, the duchess on an easel, all around you. "SURPRISE!"
- **Ending** (45–61 s): "Nobody remembered your birthday. Except the museum." You blow out the candle. Dawn on the floor plan, sunlight in the skylights: everything is back in its place, except that the dinosaur is wearing a party hat. "Nothing ever happens here." Title.

## 3. Benchmark

- *Wallace & Gromit: The Wrong Trousers* (Aardman, 1993), the night museum sequence: **learn** suspense staged with a few small lights in a dark model set, comic timing built on held stillness. **Don't take** characters, designs, shots or music.
- *Toy Story* (Pixar, 1995): **learn** the rule that things come alive only when nobody is looking, and freeze the instant someone turns round. **Don't take** characters, designs, shots or music.
- Not the Hollywood "museum comes alive" films: no dinosaur playing fetch, no named historical figures.

## 4. Characters and sets

- **You**: the guard. **Lime-green** cap and jacket (the hero colour; no set uses green), dark trousers, a torch in the right hand. Poses: walk (4), stand, look back, shock, talk, run (4), stand in the dark, blow, asleep with a party hat.
- **The Dinosaur**: a big bone-white skeleton (≈4.6 m long sprite) on a low plinth, head to the right. Poses: stand, head turned to you, frozen mid-step (one foot up, leaning in), head down to you (party), party hat.
- **The Duchess** (and her neighbours the Captain and the Cat): portraits in gold frames on the gallery wall; eyes follow the light, mouths move. At the party the Duchess sits on an easel.
- **The Mummy**: in a painted sarcophagus. Poses: sit up, yawn, talk, lie back; at the party standing, holding a cake with one candle.
- **Set: a small museum with its ceiling off.** West to east along one corridor line: the guard desk (lamp, monitor, coffee), the Hall of Giants (marble chequerboard floor, the dinosaur, two skylight shafts), the Portrait Gallery (parquet, burgundy wall, brass picture lights), the Egypt Room (sandstone, columns, the sarcophagus, two dim amber lamps). Partition walls stop short of the front so the high camera sees into every room.
- **Light**: ambient cold moonlight blue through skylights (fog the same blue); practicals: the torch (white-warm, the story light), the desk lamp, picture lights (brass), Egypt lamps (amber), red EXIT signs, display spots (warm white, popping on at the turn), the candle. Dawn: pale gold sun in the skylights.

## 7. Cue map

- **Tempo**: 120 bpm, 4/4 (bar 2 s) for the tiptoe march; the party waltz in 3/4 at the same crotchet.
- **Instruments**: pizzicato bass and pizzicato strings (pluck.py), a reed "bassoon" for the sneaking tune, music box (celesta) for the magic, harp, recorder, a synth horn, soft timpani; party: guitar strum, recorder, tambourine.
- **Sections**: intro hum (0–0.5) · tiptoe A (0.5–12.5, freezes 10.0–11.0, dead stop 12.5) · scurry (15.5–17) · tiptoe B (17–28) · Egypt drone + music box (28–37) · torch buzz, silence + heartbeat (39–42) · three harp plucks with the spots (42, 42.5, 43) · waltz (43.5–50.5) · dawn reprise (51.5–56.5) · title chord (56.5).
- Cards, banners, dialogue boxes, the head-turns, the spots, SURPRISE, the puff and the title all land on beats; `tools/cuecheck.py` checks them.
