# You Are a Hotel Receptionist — treatment

Style: 2.5D Pixel Art (`styles/hd-2d/STYLE.md`). Vertical 1080×1920, 60 fps, English voice and subtitles, 42 s.
Brief (Pixel Life, Job #4, autonomous run): you, in the second person, work one shift at a hotel front desk. Follow `ideas/FORMULA.md` §3:
- the recurring hero (the lime jacket, navy cap and face from Job #3, now a bellhop pillbox cap);
- the recurring Customer, Manager and Cat introduced here;
- a cold open on the customer's line, with text and voice on frame 0;
- a quest banner and HUD gags;
- YOU'RE FIRED;
- the last frame matches the first, and the last line loops into the first;
- one honest question;
- a cover with a 2–4 word caption.

No logo, no storyboard stop.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. The guest book.** Each disaster is a page of the guest register that you sign in. *Rejected: it's a list again (film 1), and slow to open.*

**B. The doll's house (chosen).** The hotel is a doll's-house cutaway with its front wall off: the lobby on the ground floor and room 7 (the honeymoon suite) directly above.
- The camera stays in the lobby, at the desk.
- It cranes up through the floor to room 7 when the phone rings, and drops back down when the bus arrives.
- Every disaster arrives through one of the lobby's three "doors": the guest at the desk, the phone line to upstairs, and the revolving door.
- The hotel's key rack is the meter: 8 hooks, keys going one by one, the last key the climax.

**C. The clock-in to clock-out shift.** A wall clock across a day. *Rejected: film 2 was a countdown.*

Why B:
- One set fits the 42 s length and 3 render parts.
- The doll's-house crane is the style's native move here: one continuous diorama, two floors stacked, the tilt-shift band sliding up the building.
- The key rack makes progress readable with the sound off.

Opening image from the topic: a hand hammering the desk bell, DING DING DING, and a dialogue box already open.

Score shape from the topic: a bouncy lobby bossa (brushed snare, upright bass, vibraphone) that gets more frantic. A hotel elevator chime is the motif: the chime plays at every banner. It stops dead for YOU'RE FIRED, then a game-over jingle.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** On your first shift on the front desk, a guest demands a sea view (the window faces a wall), a goat moves into the honeymoon suite, and a forty-person tour group arrives for the hotel's last key, which the goat eats.

- **Cold open / setup** (0–5 s):
  - the bell hammered, THE CUSTOMER: "I want a room with a sea view.";
  - narrator: "You are a hotel receptionist.";
  - QUEST banner: "JOB #4 · CHECK IN EVERY GUEST".
- **Disaster 1** (5–12 s): you open the curtain behind the desk: a brick wall. Patience bar drains. You hang the seascape painting from the lobby wall over the window. "...Acceptable." +10 XP, key 1 off the rack.
- **Disaster 2** (12–22.5 s): the phone, ROOM 7: "There's a goat in our honeymoon suite." Crane up: a goat on the heart-shaped bed, eating the curtain. YOU: "We don't have a goat." The goat: "MEH." It eats the Do Not Disturb sign.
- **Disaster 3** (22.5–31 s): a bus horn; crane down. Alert: TOUR GROUP · 40 GUESTS. The revolving door spins them in, camera flashes on the beat, the counter runs 1/40 → 40/40. GUIDE: "Forty rooms, please." One key left on the rack. You hold it up... the goat comes out of the lift and eats it. Silence.
- **The fall** (31–38 s):
  - THE MANAGER: "Who checked a GOAT in?";
  - card GAME OVER · YOU'RE FIRED;
  - XP runs to −999;
  - the question: "Receptionists: what's the strangest thing a guest asked for?"
- **Loop** (38–42 s):
  - the goat stands behind the desk in your pillbox cap; the Manager nods: "You're hired.";
  - the Cat sleeps on the guest book;
  - the Customer bursts back in, DING DING: "Excuse me! I want a ro—". Hard cut to frame 0: "I want a room with a sea view."

## 3. Benchmark

- *Fawlty Towers* (BBC, 1975): **learn** escalation in one hotel lobby, the desk as a stage, a calm manager line that ends you. **Don't take** characters, lines, designs or the music.
- *Epic NPC Man* (Viva La Dirt League): **learn** game logic applied to a service job: the counter NPC and the impossible customer. **Don't take** characters, lines or designs.
- The game grammar (quest banner, HP-style patience bar, XP, GAME OVER) is generic RPG language, drawn our own way. No existing game's UI, fonts or sounds.

## 4. Characters and set

- **You** (the recurring hero, Job #4):
  - look: the Job #3 face, a lime-green jacket (the hero colour; no set uses green), a navy pillbox cap with a gold band, dark trousers, a gold name tag;
  - poses: stand, talk, shocked, look back, reach up (hanging the painting), phone, hold key up, run, dejected (fired).
- **The Customer** (recurring): a short stout figure in a purple coat, big round sunglasses and a pink handbag, with a red "!" bubble. Poses: hammer bell, demand (arm up), arms crossed, satisfied.
- **The Manager** (recurring): tall, grey suit, red tie, clipboard, bald with a neat grey fringe. Poses: stand, point, nod.
- **The Cat** (recurring): a small orange cat. Poses: sleep on the guest book, tail flick, yawn.
- **The Goat**: white with small horns and a beard. Poses: stand, chew (2 frames), MEH (mouth open), walk. At the end it wears your pillbox cap.
- **The newlyweds** (room 7): a bride in white and a groom in black, sitting on the bed's edge, holding each other (one frame each and a "shocked" frame).
- **Tourists**:
  - 40 at the bus, about 14 visible in the lobby;
  - one sprite in 4 colour variants: a sun hat or cap, a camera, a backpack;
  - a GUIDE with a yellow flag on a stick.
- **Set: a doll's-house hotel, front wall off.**
  - Ground floor: the lobby.
    - Burgundy and gold carpet, cream wallpaper with a stripe, brass trims.
    - The reception desk of dark wood, with the bell, a guest book, a phone, and a lamp with a green-free (amber) shade.
    - The key rack of 8 hooks behind the desk.
    - A curtained window behind the desk.
    - A seascape painting on the left wall.
    - A brass revolving door at the right, the lift with a floor needle at the back left, a chandelier, a luggage cart, a vase of red flowers.
  - First floor: room 7, the honeymoon suite. Pink wallpaper, a heart-shaped bed, a curtain, a window, a DO NOT DISTURB sign on the door handle, rose petals.
  - Outside, at the right: a pavement, a palm-less street lamp, the tour bus pulling in.
- **Light**:
  - warm late-afternoon hotel interior, the chandelier as the key practical;
  - window light from the right through the revolving door, a pink lamp upstairs;
  - camera flashes (white pops) from the tour group;
  - the GAME OVER moment: the room light dims to red.

## 7. Cue map

- **Tempo**: 120 bpm, 4/4, bar 2 s.
- **Instruments**:
  - lobby bossa: upright bass pizz, brushed snare and shaker, nylon guitar comp, vibraphone (music_box/harp presets with a soft tremolo), muted horn stabs;
  - room 7: a harp gliss and a slow pad (honeymoon);
  - tour group: the same bossa, doubled tempo feel (16th shakers) and horn stabs on each flash;
  - game-over: a 4-note descending square-ish jingle (pluck + simple synth).
- **Sections**:
  - bell hits (0–2);
  - bossa A (2.5–6.0), dead stop at the brick wall 6.0;
  - gull and waves under the hanging, then the bossa back at 11.5;
  - phone (12.0) with the bossa thinned;
  - honeymoon harp (15.5–22.5);
  - bus horn (22.5), bossa B frantic (23–30);
  - silence (30.5–31.5);
  - Manager line over a held low note;
  - game over (34.0);
  - bossa A reprise (38–42), cut mid-phrase for the loop.
- **On beats**: the bell hits, the banners, every dialogue box, the curtain, the painting, XP, the cranes, the flashes, the lift ding, the crunch, the card, the hire, the last DINGs. `tools/cuecheck.py` checks them.

## 9. Type, subtitles, HUD

- The channel UI: Cinzel caps in gold for banners; Cormorant Garamond dialogue boxes with name plates (lower third); italic narration subtitles.
- The game HUD (new for this series, same every episode, our own design):
  - **XP** counter top left;
  - **KEYS 8/8** top right (a little key glyph);
  - a **patience bar** over the Customer (red segments);
  - **+10 XP** / **−1 KEY** pop-ups;
  - the GAME OVER card: dark red plate, "GAME OVER" small, "YOU'RE FIRED" big, Cinzel.
  - The HUD is drawn in pixels: a 2-px gold frame and block segments.
- QUEST banner text: "JOB #4 · CHECK IN EVERY GUEST". Alert: "TOUR GROUP · 40 GUESTS".
- The question is on screen 35–38 s under the card, and pinned.
- Subtitles exported as `subtitles_en.srt` (captions burned in).
- **Cover** (thumb): a tight crop of you behind the desk with the goat chewing the last key, caption "ROOM 7: GOAT".
