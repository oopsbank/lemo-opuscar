# Midnight Crossing (Chuyến tàu lúc 00:00) — treatment

The user's own script, 65 s, 9:16, wordless, Tilt-Shift Miniature style. The script fixes the story, the layout and the beats; this
file records how they are staged.

Logline: at midnight a toy level crossing rings for a train no one expects; a ghost train takes a man in a yellow raincoat and leaves his shadow behind.

## Three structures considered
1. **Locked wide all the way (the script's own grammar)**: one composition, three layers (apartments / crossing / pavement); the camera only breathes, pushes in, shakes and makes the final glance. Every strange thing happens somewhere in the same frame, so it rewards rewatching (the easter eggs). **Chosen.**
2. Cut to inserts (clock CU, shadow CU, coat CU): clearer, but breaks the "living diorama" and the "spot the 4 strange things" game.
3. Start on the manhole and pull out (a loop): spoils the last beat.

## Staging (world metres, x east, z south toward the camera; rails along z = 0)
- Top third: two 4-storey blocks with stained facades (6–7 warm windows lit), the street running north between them; roofs with inox tanks, a slow AC fan, laundry, 8 pigeons on the east parapet; a vertical neon HOTEL sign top right; a balcony on floor 3 (west block); a curtain blowing on floor 3 (east block).
- Middle third: the level crossing (rails, two yellow/black barriers, two signal posts), the north–south street crossing an east–west street just south of the rails. Noodle cart with an orange tarp, 2 tables, 3 diners, steam (left); glass bus shelter with the girl in red and a clear umbrella, a cyan vending machine (right).
- Bottom third: tiled pavement, a tall amber street lamp and its pool, a cast-iron manhole (bottom right), a convenience store (bottom left) with a red LED clock 23:59.
- Light: cold blue night, warm pools (lamp, cart, store), wet road with long reflections under every light, drizzle visible in the lamp light.

## Beats (film seconds)
0–12 normal life: drizzle, traffic crossing slowly, the cook ladling, the girl shaking her umbrella, the yellow raincoat walking from the store across the zebra and up to the rails.
12 the clock turns 00:00 → signals flash, bell, barriers drop (CLACK) with the raincoat trapped between them, traffic stops, pigeons burst off, the steam dies, the cook freezes.
24–38 slow push-in; the shadow on the wet road stretches like ink and crawls to the girl; she steps back and drops her umbrella; a figure comes out on the floor-3 balcony and stares. Bell slows and sinks, a low drone.
38–52 a cold headlight from the left; a translucent ghost train full of still black passengers rushes through; puddles burst; the umbrella rolls; the man flickers out frame by frame, the raincoat drops flat; his shadow stands up.
52–65 signals off, barriers up, the lamp blinks twice, traffic and the cook carry on (cars swerve round the coat); the shadow crosses, picks up the umbrella, hands it to the girl (she nods), walks off among the pedestrians; a quick glance to the manhole: the lid slides, two white eyes look at the coat. Black.

Sound: rain bed, city hum, sizzle; bell KENG-KENG, barrier CLACK, traffic cut to rain only; the bell detunes and slows, drone; an underwater whistle, wheels RẦM-RẦM, rumble; two lamp pops, traffic back; a tiny KÉT. A sparse, low original score from CC0 samples.
