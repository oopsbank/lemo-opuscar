# Dimples — treatment (Toon on Set, ~30 s, 1080×1920, 24 fps, English)

Topic: **Why do golf balls have dimples?** Answer (true): the dimples stir up a thin layer of turbulent air that hugs the ball longer, so the wake behind it is much smaller and the ball has less drag; with backspin, the dimples also help it lift. A dimpled ball flies roughly twice as far as a smooth one. (Golfers once noticed that old, scuffed balls flew farther than new, smooth ones.)

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Fixing what isn't broken (chosen).** A fussy golfer sands his ball perfectly smooth ("Much better."). His swing: the ball wobbles a few metres and plops. The caddie gives him a normal ball, and the narrator shows why in a smoke-trail insert. The second swing flies out of sight. Button: the golfer, now a believer, wears a dimpled hat and tries to fly. Nothing.
2. **The race.** Two golfers hit at once, one with a smooth ball and one with a dimpled ball; the camera follows both balls. It's clear, but one joke is stretched over 30 s.
3. **Three wrong guesses.** For grip? (he licks it.) For decoration? (he paints it.) To count the holes? (he counts.) Then the real answer. It's funny, but there's no demonstration of the physics: the answer would be only told.

**Why 1:** it has a demonstration with a consequence and a readable thumbnail gag (a sad ball plopping off the tee), an explaining insert that only this medium can do (smoke trails around a ball in a lit set), and an escalation button instead of the crackers film's "do it right" ending. The opening is a close-up of sandpaper on a ball, not the counter or a flag.

## 2. Logline and arc

A golfer who "fixes" his ball by sanding off its dimples learns why they're there.

- **Setup:** golden-hour tee box. Sanding, polishing, "Much better."
- **Turn:** the smooth ball plops. The insert: a smooth ball drags a huge swirling wake; a dimpled ball leaves a small, tidy one.
- **Ending:** a real ball flies out of sight. He puts on a dimpled hat and jumps. Nothing. The caddie, deadpan: "Doesn't work on people."

## 4. Look and sound

- **Set:** a golf course at golden hour. A tee box (cut grass with stripes, a tee marker, a ball washer), rolling fairway, a sand bunker, trees in the far blur, a flag far away, long shadows. Low sun from front-left.
- **Cast:**
  - the golfer: a plaid flat cap, a teal argyle sweater vest, a mustache;
  - the caddie: an orange polo, a white visor, a ponytail, a bag of clubs.
- **Smoke insert:** a dark backdrop board on the tee (a demo rig the caddie set up). Smoke lines are Puffs and ribbon lines in the set's light.
- **Score:** 120 BPM pizzicato, plus a jaunty clarinet and ukulele. It stops dead for the plop; a "wah-wah" bassoon; a marimba under the explanation; a big xylophone run up with the long drive; a deadpan two-note button.
- **Foley:** sandpaper, a polish squeak, the club swoosh, a ball "tink" vs "CRACK", a plop, grass, a cricket, birds, the hat jump thud.
- **Voices:**
  - narrator `am_michael`;
  - golfer `bm_george` (pompous), to check with ASR;
  - caddie `af_nicole` or `bf_emma` (deadpan), to check with ASR.
